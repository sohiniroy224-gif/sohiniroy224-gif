## 🚀 Projects

### 🗺️ [AirIQ](https://github.com/sohiniroy224-gif) — Full-Stack Air Quality Management 
> *Project Description*

AirIQ is an interactive, data-driven air quality and public health analytics platform designed to monitor, analyze, and predict air pollution levels across different cities.

The platform provides real-time air quality insights, historical pollution trends, city-wise comparisons, and predictive analytics to help users understand environmental conditions and their potential impact on public health.

Using data analytics, machine learning, and interactive visualizations, AirIQ transforms complex environmental data into meaningful insights. It helps users identify pollution patterns, understand major pollutants such as PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, and CO, and make informed decisions regarding environmental and health awareness.



**Tech Stack:** React.js • Tailwind CSS • Node.js • Express • MongoDB  • JWT • SendGrid

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/🌐_Live_Demo-TripCalculator-4ea94b?style=for-the-badge)](https://tripcalculator.in)

## Key Features

Real-time Air Quality Monitoring

Predictive Air Quality Analytics

Interactive Maps and City Rankings

Historical Pollution Data Analysis

City-wise Air Quality Comparison

Public Health Recommendations

AI-powered Assistant

User Authentication and Saved Cities

Power BI Data Visualization
